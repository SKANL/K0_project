# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone participating in K0, regardless of experience level, identity, background, disability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and outcomes rather than people.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and confidential information.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive conduct are not tolerated. Do not use project spaces to solicit, distribute, or expose credentials, customer data, or exploit details.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project events, and other spaces where someone represents K0.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](./.github/SECURITY.md), not a public report.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and may take action ranging from a private warning to removal from project spaces. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not tolerated.
